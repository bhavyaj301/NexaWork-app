1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PLATFORM OVERVIEW
================================================================================
NexaWork AI is an enterprise-grade SaaS intelligence web platform designed to analyze 
over 2.48 million live and historical technology job postings in real time.

Key Platform Objectives:
- Deliver macro hiring demand trends, compensation benchmarks, and layoff recovery curves.
- Provide geographical salary mapping and cost-of-living purchasing power comparisons.
- Enable interactive skill co-occurrence network graphs and certification ROI modeling.
- Offer automated AI Resume Upload & ATS Parsing with quantified bullet-point rewrites.
- Provide an in-place floating AI Copilot modal for contextual career coaching.
- Secure single-admin master access with 2FA, encrypted local logging, and auth gating.

3. CORE INTELLIGENCE MODULES & SERVICES (9 MODULES)
================================================================================
NexaWork AI features 9 dedicated full-featured intelligence modules in ServicesHub:

1. AI Resume Analyzer & Matcher (NEW):
   - Upload PDF/DOCX/TXT/MD resumes, parse technical stacks, calculate ATS fit scores, 
     rewrite weak bullet points with quantified metrics, and model salary leap.

2. Market Trends & Forecasting (Macro Analytics):
   - Longitudinal hiring velocity, layoff recovery curves, hiring growth (+18.4% YoY),
     and sector-by-sector hiring surge breakdowns.

3. Global Salaries by City (Geographic Intelligence):
   - 12 major tech metros (San Francisco, New York, Seattle, London, Bangalore, etc.) 
     comparing median base compensation, tax brackets, and cost-of-living indices.

4. Skill Co-occurrence Graph (Skill Intelligence):
   - Physics-based interactive network graph visualizing co-requisite cloud stacks 
     and high-demand skill clustering.

5. Cloud Certification ROI (Career Value):
   - Calculates median salary lift and time-to-payback for industry credentials 
     (Solutions Architect, DevOps, Data Engineering, Zero Trust Security).

6. AI Career Gap Diagnostic (AI Career Pathway):
   - Gap matching against target roles with custom 12-week roadmap generation.

7. Cognitive Job Explorer (Job Matching):
   - Semantic job search engine with 1-click tailored interview question generator 
     and custom cover letter generator.

8. Predictive Salary Simulator (Compensation Modeling):
   - Parametric multi-variable compensation calculator factoring in experience level,
     specialization, company tier, equity grants, and annual bonuses.

9. Cloud Data Topology (Cloud Engineering):
   - Real-time streaming pipeline telemetry visualizer (Event Ingestion, Stream Analytics, 
     Vector Search, NoSQL Data Store) with live event generation.

6. LOCAL DATABASE & AUDIT LOGGING ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
Files:
- src/data/database.json (Full user records, notification logs, audit history)
- src/data/user_login_logout_data.json (Dedicated password-protected auth records)

Key Mechanics:
- Every LOGIN, LOGOUT, SIGNUP, 2FA_VERIFICATION, and BLOCKED_ATTEMPT event is 
  automatically recorded with timestamp, user email, IP address, device, and status.
- Frontend triggers POST /api/sync-database on Vite server to persist state to disk.
- User Data Viewer Modal (src/components/auth/UserDataViewerModal.tsx):
  * Accessible from Navbar User Profile dropdown.
  * Protected with master password "bhavya@123".
  * Decrypts live audit records in-app with search, event filters, and CSV export.
